# Event and prize research

Verified against official pages on 9 October 2026. Host-specific announcements
take precedence; do not claim unverified organizer approval.

The [event](https://events.mlh.com/events/14886-hacktoberfest-hack-day-x-cycoders-club)
is Hacktoberfest Hack Day Barasat x CYCODERS Club at Adamas University. Its schedule
lists coding blocks totaling about 5h55m, submission 5:45–6:15 PM and demos
6:15–7:15 PM. Header and schedule end times differ; confirm onsite timing.

The [event challenges](https://www.mlh.com/events/hacktoberfest-hack-day-x-cycoders-club/challenges)
list **Best Use of Gemma 4** and **Best Open-Source AI Project**.
[MLH's open-source AI guidance](https://www.mlh.com/opensource-ai) calls for meaningful
AI use, a public repository, an open-source license and reproducible setup/model
documentation. Kin can satisfy this after a real working Gemma extraction demo;
repository visibility alone does not establish prize eligibility or winning quality.
Gemma 4's [model card](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core/model_card_4) identifies
Apache 2.0 model licensing. Open weights are not evidence of clinical suitability.

The [standard MLH rules](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/standard-hackathon-rules.md)
permit planning before the event, generally require building during the event,
and require honest disclosure of tools/AI assistance and reused components. Follow
the host's actual rules on pre-existing code and submission formats. No user data,
credentials, copied proprietary material or misleading demo claims in submissions.

## Credits
The user reports Render $50, DigitalOcean $25, Backboard $5 and Tinker $10.
Redemption, account balance and expiry were not inspected. The public MLH offer
excludes proprietary DO inference models; link activation/expiry is distinct from
the lifetime of an account credit balance. Verify the actual redeemed offer.

Render static hosting and Supabase's limited free tier fit the MVP. Use optional
DigitalOcean credits for Gemma extraction after confirming model/image access.
No paid Render API, Backboard memory, extra OCR, embeddings or Tinker training are
required. Tinker has inference/training capabilities, but this MVP needs no fine-tuning.
