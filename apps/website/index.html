A medical invitation

Private family access binds to one invited Google account. Selected information only, for 24 hours from link creation.

⌁

An opt-in emergency medical QR

Enable it explicitly to let anyone with the QR open selected medicines, allergies, notes and reviewed lab results without login. Originals and Drive links stay private. Rotate or revoke access at any time.

All browser routes need internet. An emergency QR does not verify doctor identity; anyone holding or copying it can view your selected snapshot.

A boundary worth keeping

Permission is part
of the product.

01

Private by default.

Original images, extraction drafts and full history stay separate from recipient responses. Server-side ownership checks enforce access.

02

Specific access.

Medical invitations bind to the invited account. Expiry and revocation are checked on every read. Contact cards and owner-enabled emergency medical QR links are intentionally public to their holders.

03

Clear limits.

Revocation cannot erase a copy already made. Kin’s server and AI provider can process plaintext; we do not claim end-to-end encryption.

Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Uninstall older debug-signed previews first. The signed 0.1.3 and 0.1.4 hosted builds can update to 0.2.0. Removing an app clears its local data.

Release notes & checksums ↗

A few practical questions

Know what you’re opening.

Is this ready for real patient records?+

No. Kin is a hackathon prototype. Use fictional records. Hosted authorization and live AI extraction checks have passed; The physical-device Google login walkthrough and production operational review remain incomplete.

Does the recipient need to install Kin?+

No. All recipient routes open in a standard browser. Private invitations require the invited Google account. Contact cards and explicitly enabled emergency medical QR links require no login.

Does AI decide what medicines I should take?+

No. AI extracts private draft fields from prescriptions and laboratory reports. You review it against the original. Kin does not diagnose, predict health risks, check drug interactions or recommend treatment.

Can I use it without the development laptop?+

The 0.2.0 APK connects directly to hosted Supabase and the browser receiver. It needs an internet connection. Google sign-in is configured; the physical-device login walkthrough remains pending.

What happens when I revoke access?+

The service denies subsequent reads of a revoked medical invitation. A recipient may already have copied the information. Public contact cards and emergency medical QR links can also be revoked or rotated.

Can Kin connect to any laboratory portal?+

Direct public HTTPS report-file links can be imported. Dr Lal PathLabs requires a Lab/Visit ID and password: open its portal, download your report and import the PDF. Kin does not bypass logins or automatically access private Drive content.
